# Future Job Market (2026–2030)

This project analyzes the future labor market trends between 2026 and 2030, focusing on:
- 📊 **High-demand jobs** (Cyber Security, Data Science, Healthcare, Engineering, etc.)
- 🛠️ **Key skills** required for IT, healthcare, and industrial growth
- 💰 **Salary projections** with regional comparisons
- 🌍 **Regional opportunities** in Cairo, Alexandria, New Capital, Smart Village, and industrial zones
- 📑 **System analysis & design** with UML, ER, and DFD diagrams
- 🖥️ **Implementation & testing** supported by structured workflows

## Deliverables
- Research report (PDF format)
- Salary tables & KPI dashboards
- Technical documentation & user manual

## Purpose
To provide insights evolving job market, helping students, professionals, and policymakers understand future opportunities and required skills.
